GUS MODE

Daily GTA 6 news with Gus

Gus Mode is a GTA 6 news channel hosted by Gus, an animated gator. Every day we publish a few short news videos and one longer round-up on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

How we make it: our own software finds the day's GTA 6 news, writes a script, has every claim checked against its sources, and turns it into a video with Gus. Rumours are labelled as rumours, and we never show spoilers, mod menus or money glitches. The channel owner reviews the videos, and they are published only to our own accounts.
