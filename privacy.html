Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Gus Mode publishing app ("the app"): the software the Gus Mode channel uses to publish its own videos. It is used only by the channel owner, on the channel's own accounts. It has no sign-up and no other users.

What the app connects to

With the channel owner's permission, the app connects to the Gus Mode accounts on YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta). It uses those connections to:

  • upload and publish our own videos, with their titles, descriptions and captions;
  • check that an upload worked;
  • read view counts and other statistics of our own videos, to learn what our viewers like;
  • read public comments on our own videos to find news topics viewers ask for.

What we keep

  • Login tokens for our own accounts, stored encrypted. They are used only by the app.
  • Upload records: which video went to which platform, its link and its status.
  • Statistics about our own videos (views, watch time and similar numbers).
  • Topic requests from comments: comments are reduced to short topic phrases, such as "map size". We don't keep usernames, profile information or the comments themselves, and we never contact commenters through the app.

Who processes data

The app runs on GitHub (Microsoft). To turn comments into topic phrases and to write and check scripts, text is processed by Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI (ChatGPT). Notifications to the channel owner go through Telegram. Data from YouTube, TikTok and Instagram is used only for the purposes above. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. You can remove the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Keeping and deleting

Upload records and statistics about our own videos are kept so we can compare how our videos do. Login tokens are deleted when access is removed. To ask about your data or to have something deleted, contact us below.
